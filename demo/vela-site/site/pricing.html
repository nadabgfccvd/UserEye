FAQ

Questions, answered honestly

What counts as an “editor”?

Anyone who creates dashboards, alerts or queries. Viewers who only look at dashboards and get paged by alerts are free and unlimited on every plan.

What happens if we exceed our ingest?

Nothing breaks and nothing is silently dropped. We keep ingesting, tell you within the hour, and bill overage at $0.20/GB — or you can set a hard cap in the workspace settings.

Can we self-host Vela?

Yes — the Business plan includes a Kubernetes Helm chart for a fully air-gapped deployment, with the same feature set as the cloud.

Do you discount for startups and non-profits?

50% off the first year for companies under 20 people and registered non-profits. Email us from your work address.

How is Vela cheaper than the incumbents?

Columnar storage plus a Rust query core means we index less and compress harder. We pass the infrastructure savings on instead of charging per host, per span and per dashboard seat.

Still unsure? Talk to an engineer.

Not a salesperson — an actual product engineer who will estimate your ingest and tell you if we're the wrong tool.
