Privacy

We collect the minimum needed to run the service: account details, billing records, and the telemetry you choose to send us. This demo site itself sets no cookies — it stores your theme and consent choice in local storage only, and nothing ever leaves your browser.

  • No third-party trackers, analytics beacons or ad pixels.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data, ever.
  • EU or US data residency on the Business plan; encryption at rest with customer-managed keys available.
  • Deletion means deletion: purged from primary storage within 30 days and from backups within 90.

Terms

The real terms are 14 pages. The honest summary: you own your data; we get a limited licence to process it to provide the service; either side can terminate with 30 days' notice; our liability is capped at 12 months of fees, except where the law says otherwise. If a clause in the real document contradicts this summary, the real document wins — and we would like an email about it, because that means the summary is lying.

DPA

Our Data Processing Addendum incorporates the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (2021/914, Module 2) and the UK Addendum. Sub-processor list is published with a 30-day advance-notice obligation for changes. Objections are handled by privacy@vela.example within 10 business days.

Security & SLA

SOC 2 Type II report available under NDA. Penetration tests run twice a year by an independent firm; executive summaries are shared with Business customers. Vulnerability disclosure: security@vela.example (PGP key on request), 24-hour acknowledgement target, safe-harbour for good-faith research.

The Business plan carries a 99.98% monthly uptime SLA with service credits: 10% below 99.98%, 25% below 99.9%, 50% below 99.5%. Status history for the last 90 days is published at the homepage status pill (demo).
