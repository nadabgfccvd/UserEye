Story

Seven years, one obsession

Making telemetry fast enough that people actually use it.

  1. 2019

    Maya and Rafael build the first prototype in a Lisbon coworking space. The pitch: “traces that open before you finish clicking”.

  2. 2021

    First 1,000 teams. Series A. The agent that auto-discovers your stack ships — onboarding drops from two weeks to ten minutes.

  3. 2023

    Anomaly detection goes GA. For the first time, Vela pages customers about problems before their customers notice them.

  4. 2025

    The columnar re-architecture lands. 10 trillion events a day, p99 queries under two seconds, storage costs down 63%.

  5. 2026

    SOC 2 Type II, EU data residency and DPA signed. 4,200+ teams, 84 people, profitable and remote-first.

Values

What we actually mean by them

Not posters on a wall — these decide who we hire, what we ship and which customers we say no to.

Speed is a feature

Every pull request runs the full query benchmark. If a change makes Vela slower, it does not merge — no matter whose change it is.

Privacy is architecture

We cannot leak what we do not store. Telemetry stays in your region, keys stay in your hands, and we publish our sub-processors.

Write it down

Decisions live in public RFCs, incidents in blameless postmortems, roadmaps in the open. Async-first beats meeting-first.
